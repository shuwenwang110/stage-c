Stage C brings together the set, lighting and costumes for my design of The Caucasian Chalk Circle at the Sumner Theatre.

Stage design / Set, light & costume / 2026

Stage C

The Caucasian Chalk Circle

I imagine villagers putting on a play with whatever materials they can find after war. They move the blocks and turn the panels in front of the audience. The same pieces become a home, a dangerous bridge and the circle used in the final trial.

Bertolt Brecht / translated by Alistair Beaton
Sumner Theatre / 1:50 scale-model proposal

01 / Set & lighting

Set & lighting storyboard

Fifteen moments cover the prologue and all five acts. Small areas of light, low side light and strong shadows change the focus as the scenery is rearranged. The same blocks form both the bridge and the trial circle, with old wood and patchwork curtains connecting the scenes.

02 / Costume design

The people and their roles

Ordinary work clothes belong to the villagers telling the story. Masks and exaggerated shapes distinguish the characters they perform. Patches, worn fabric and faded colours connect the costumes to the reused scenery.
